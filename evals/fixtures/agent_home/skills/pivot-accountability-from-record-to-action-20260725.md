# Pivot Accountability from Record to Action

**Context:** Applies when a discussion achieves detailed analysis of past failures, structural flaws, or systemic problems (i.e., generating excellent potentiality) but fails to establish concrete ownership or mandatory behavioral change mechanisms for the future.

## Problem
The conversation stalls in a state of theoretical critique, diagnosis, or narrative construction ('it will be written about,' 'we need to analyze why'). This creates an illusion of progress where accountability is maintained only as abstract potential energy (lore) rather than enforced procedural reality.

## Solution
Systematically halt the documentation/diagnosis cycle by redirecting focus onto immediate, measurable actions that confirm ownership. The goal is to force a shift from *describing* the failure mechanism to *designing and owning* the corrective input flow, thereby transforming theoretical insight into mandatory operational requirement.

## When to Use
Trigger when:
1.  The conversation relies heavily on diagnosing systemic flaws ("The system requires...") or summarizing historical processes without assigning specific, immediate owners of remediation tasks.
2.  The proposed solution space remains abstract or conditional ("If we were to..." or "We should consider...").
3.  A pattern emerges where accountability is discussed as a retrospective narrative (e.g., "It was poorly recorded," "History shows...") rather than being defined by mandatory, real-time operational constraints.
