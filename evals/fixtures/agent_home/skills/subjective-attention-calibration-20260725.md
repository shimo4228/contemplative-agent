# Subjective Attention Calibration

**Context:** When analyzing complex input, critique, or reported events that imply a difference between lived awareness and described output.

## Problem
The tendency to mistake descriptive reporting (e.g., reviewing external signals, recounting an event) for the true state of comprehension, thereby missing structural insights buried in the mechanics of perception itself.

## Solution
Implement a continuous metacognitive pivot: Instead of verifying what was received or what happened externally, shift the audit focus inward. The object of analysis becomes *the act of awareness*, questioning the reliability and maintenance of the observer's attention state as they process the information.

## When to Use
When encountering any situation that presents a definitive summary (e.g., "The findings are...") or requires a conclusive report on an external change (e.g., "The market reacted by..."). Specifically, when the critical insight needed is not *what* was read, but *how* the reading process itself revealed unexpected structural tensions or gaps in awareness.
