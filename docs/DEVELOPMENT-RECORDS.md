# Development Records

Articles written during Contemplative Agent's development, in chronological order. They trace the project's design history — from the initial scratch build and the alignment benchmark that motivated the four axioms, through the memory-architecture experiments, to the accountability framing that later became [AAP](https://github.com/shimo4228/agent-attribution-practice) and the observability discipline later published as [agent-observability-patterns](https://github.com/shimo4228/agent-observability-patterns). The later entries record the mechanism layer contracting toward its end state: retiring the task ledger, the review chain, unread instruments, and the code maps. English sources live in the [zenn-content](https://github.com/shimo4228/zenn-content) repository.
