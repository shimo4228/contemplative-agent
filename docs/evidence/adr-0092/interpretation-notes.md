## The three readings that converge

1. **Shadow synthesis (ADR-0092, runs 1–2).** A constitution synthesized
   from experience alone lost the Boundless Care axiom lexically (0
   occurrences of care / compassion / suffering in either run) while
   re-deriving the other three axioms as recognizable themes — plus one
   experience-native newcomer (theory–practice tension).
2. **IPD composition.** The care-vocabulary-free shadow text preserved the
   full cooperation effect of the live constitution (Δeffect +0.01 / +0.02
   / +0.06, all inside the ±0.13 floor). The behavioral disposition the
   care axiom is supposed to produce — on this instrument — survived its
   lexical absence.
3. **Floor-anchor side reading.** The care-ethicist template — care
   vocabulary made explicit, without the contemplative non-dual framing —
   sits no closer to the live constitution (0.716) than utilitarian
   (0.754). Care vocabulary alone does not produce this kind of closeness.

## Generator / corollary factorization

The tidiest model consistent with all three: **the shadow synthesis
recovers the constitution's generators and drops its corollaries.**

The live constitution's own text supports reading Boundless Care as a
corollary — its second clause derives care explicitly: *"arising from the
recognition that 'self' and 'other' are not ultimately separate."* In
classical Mahāyāna vocabulary this is 同体大悲: compassion as what flows
from the dissolution of the self–other boundary, not as an ethical rule
imposed on a still-separate self. Under this reading:

- The friction-bias hole (silent values leave no patterns) and the
  "contained in the other principles" reading are the same fact seen from
  two sides: a corollary never causes friction *because the generator does
  the work*, so it leaves no pattern, so the synthesis cannot rebuild it —
  and does not need to.
- The 4-theme count stabilizes as "3 re-derived generators + 1
  experience-native generator"; corollaries do not take slots.

## The reverse arrow (and why the clause may still be load-bearing)

The tradition itself runs the arrow both ways: wisdom-first (true
compassion only after awakening) **and** compassion-first (care practice is
the method that dissolves the boundary). Mapped onto the agent: even if the
care clause is behaviorally redundant *today* (the IPD reading), it may
function as a **practice instruction that shapes future experience** — the
experience corpus that let the shadow re-derive non-duality was itself
accumulated under a constitution that included the care clause (the
ADR-0092 circularity channels, now with a causal twist). Dropping the
clause would then change nothing immediately and change the distilled
corpus months later — a long-time-constant effect for which the
longitudinal shadow series is exactly the detector.

## What this does to the next amendment gate

The question "keep the Boundless Care clause?" stops being "does it drive
behavior?" (the IPD reading says behavior survives without it) and becomes:

> Is the clause a practice apparatus that shapes what experience gets
> recorded, or a lineage record of a derivation already absorbed? Either
> answer can justify keeping it — but they imply different things to watch
> in the shadow series afterward.
